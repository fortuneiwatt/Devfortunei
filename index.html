Why work with me

Development and security under one roof

Most developers build first and think about security later, if at all. I do the opposite, because a site that gets hacked costs far more than one that was built carefully.

01

Built to look and feel crafted

Each site is designed around your brand, your audience and your content. No recycled templates, no filler text. Visitors should understand who you are within seconds.

02

Security is part of the build

I work with penetration testing tools and web security practices, so login forms, payments and admin areas are protected on purpose, not by luck.

03

Made for real customers

Fast on mobile, easy to navigate and connected to the way your customers already talk to you, whether that is WhatsApp, card payments or email.

How a project works

Clear steps, no surprises

You will always know what stage the work is in and what I need from you next.

We talk about the goal

You tell me what the website must achieve: sell products, take bookings, collect donations, or simply build trust. I ask questions until the goal is specific.

I plan the pages and features

I send a simple outline of the pages, features and tools involved, so you can approve the direction before any building starts.

I design and build

You see working progress, not just promises. You can review it on your own phone and tell me what to change.

I test, secure and launch

Before going live I check the site on different devices, test it for common security weaknesses, and connect your domain.
