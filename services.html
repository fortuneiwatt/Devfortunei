Common questions

Before you reach out

I am not technical. Will I understand what is happening?

Yes. I explain things in plain language and avoid jargon unless you ask for the detail. You will always know what stage your project is at.

How long does a project take?

It depends on the size. A simple business site is much quicker than a store with payments and an admin area. After our first conversation I will give you a realistic timeline before you commit to anything.

What do I need to prepare?

Your logo if you have one, the text and photos you want to show, and a rough idea of what you want visitors to do. If you do not have text or photos yet, we can work out what is needed together.

Can you fix or secure a website someone else built?

Yes. I can review an existing site, test it for weaknesses, and either fix the problems or give you a clear written list of what needs attention.

Will I be able to update the site myself?

If you need to change content often, such as products or prices, I can build a simple admin area for that. If changes are rare, I can make them for you.

How do I get started?

Go to the contact page and send a short description of what you need. That is enough for me to reply with useful next steps.

Not sure which service fits?

Describe your situation in a few sentences and I will tell you what I would recommend.
