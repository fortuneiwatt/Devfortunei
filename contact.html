Contact

Let us talk about your project

You do not need a finished idea or technical words. A few honest sentences about what you want to achieve are enough to begin.

Ways to reach me

Choose whichever is easiest for you. I read every message personally.

Email Best for detailed briefs and attachments. Freelancer.com Prefer to hire through a platform with payment protection? Find me there.

What to include in your first message

These points help me reply with useful advice instead of a list of questions.

  • Who you are and what your business or organization does.
  • What you want the website or app to achieve, for example taking orders, bookings or donations.
  • Any websites you like, and anything you dislike about your current one.
  • Your ideal launch date, if you have one.
  • A rough budget range, so I can recommend what fits.
What happens next

After you send your message

I reply with questions and first thoughts

I will confirm I understood your goal and ask about anything unclear.

You receive a simple plan

Pages, features, timeline and cost, written so you can decide with confidence.

We agree and begin

Once you approve the plan, I start building and share progress as it happens.
