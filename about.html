Hello, I am Fortune

I am a freelance fullstack developer and cybersecurity specialist based in Nigeria. People know me online as DEVFORTUNE. I design and build websites and web applications, and I make sure they are protected against the kinds of attacks that regularly hit small businesses.

I work independently, which means that when you hire me, you talk directly to the person building your project. There is no hand-off to a junior team and no account manager in between.

I build things the way I would want them built for me: clear to use, honest about what they do, and hard to break.

What I do

On the development side, I work across the whole stack. I build what visitors see using React, Next.js, TypeScript and Tailwind CSS. I build what runs behind the scenes using Node.js and NestJS, and I store data in PostgreSQL. For simpler brochure-style sites, I also write clean, hand-crafted HTML and CSS, which is often the fastest and most reliable choice.

On the security side, I work with penetration testing tools and web security practices. That background changes how I build: I think about how a login page could be abused, how a payment form could be tampered with and how an admin area could be reached by the wrong person before I write the first line.

The kinds of clients I work with

My work has covered restaurants, a barbershop, boutique hotels, churches, an NGO collecting donations in several currencies, online stores, and technical products for developers. Each of these taught me something different about how real people use the web, and about what business owners actually need rather than what sounds impressive.

I also use my own projects to keep learning. I have built an AI assistant on Telegram, a Web3 tool for a blockchain hackathon, and I am developing a free online tools platform. I enjoy new technology, and I use it in client work only when it genuinely helps the client.

How I work

Plainly

I explain things in everyday language. If something is not necessary, I will tell you, even if it means a smaller project.

Carefully

Sites are tested on real devices and checked for common security flaws before they go live.

Honestly

I give realistic timelines and clear scope. If something changes, you hear about it straight away.

With craft

I care about the details: natural wording, balanced colours, comfortable spacing. A site should feel like it was made for you.

Working with me

Most clients start with a short message describing what they need. From there I reply with questions, then a plan, and you approve the plan before any building begins. During the build you see progress regularly, and after launch I remain available for questions and small fixes.

I take on projects through direct contact and through freelance platforms such as Freelancer.com, and I am comfortable working with clients in other time zones.
