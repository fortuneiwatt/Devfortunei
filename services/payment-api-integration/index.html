Common questions

Which should I use: Paystack, Stripe or PayPal?

Paystack is a common choice for customers paying in Nigeria. Stripe and PayPal are widely used for international customers. Many businesses offer more than one.

How do you keep payments secure?

Card details are handled by the payment provider rather than stored on your site, and secret keys are kept out of public code.

Can you fix an existing integration?

Yes. I can review a broken or incomplete payment setup and fix or rebuild it.
