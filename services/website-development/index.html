Common questions

How long does a business website take to build?

It depends on the number of pages and features. After a short discovery conversation I give you a clear timeline before any work begins.

Will my website appear on Google?

I build every site with search in mind: proper titles, descriptions, headings, fast loading and a sitemap. Ranking also depends on competition and time, so nobody can honestly guarantee a position.

Can I update the content myself?

Yes. If you want to edit text and images without a developer, I can connect a content management system such as Sanity or Strapi.
