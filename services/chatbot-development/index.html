Chatbots that answer customers and hand over to a person

I build chatbots for businesses that answer the questions customers ask most, such as prices and availability, and pass the conversation to a real customer care agent when a person is needed. They cut repetitive support work without leaving customers stuck.

Who this is for

  • Online shops with repeated product and price questions
  • Restaurants and service businesses
  • Companies with a customer care team
  • Websites that want instant answers outside office hours

What I build

  • Answers to common customer questions
  • Product and price lookups, for example listing goods under a chosen price
  • Handover to customer care for cases the bot should not handle
  • A chat widget on your website
  • AI language models combined with your own business information
  • Conversation records and a way to update what the bot knows

Common questions

Will the chatbot give wrong answers?

A chatbot is only as reliable as the information it is given. I base answers on your own business data and set clear rules for when it should hand over to a person instead of guessing.

Can a real person take over the conversation?

Yes. Handover to your customer care team is part of the design for cases the bot should not handle.

Can it be added to my existing website?

Yes. A chat widget can be added to an existing site or built into a new one.
