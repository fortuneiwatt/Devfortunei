Which payment gateways can you integrate?

Paystack, Stripe and PayPal. Paystack suits customers paying in Naira, while Stripe and PayPal suit international customers.

Can I manage products and orders myself?

Yes. Every store includes an admin dashboard for products, orders and customer details.

Can I sell digital products such as ebooks?

Yes. Digital downloads can be delivered automatically after a confirmed payment.
