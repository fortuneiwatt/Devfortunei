Common questions

Which tasks are worth automating?

Tasks that repeat often, follow clear rules and take real time, such as sending confirmations, updating records or producing reports.

Do I need to change the tools I use now?

Usually not. Automations are built to connect with the tools you already use through their APIs and webhooks.

Is AI always necessary?

No. Many automations work better with simple rules. I only use AI where it clearly helps, such as understanding text or documents.
