School management systems built around how your school works

I build custom school management systems that bring student records, attendance, results, fees and communication into one secure web platform. Instead of forcing your school into a fixed template, the modules are chosen and shaped with your school’s own processes.

Discuss your projectSee my work

Who this is for

  • Nursery, primary and secondary schools
  • Tutorial and training centres
  • Schools still using paper registers or spreadsheets
  • School owners who want fees and results in one place

What I build

  • Student and staff records
  • Classes, subjects and timetables
  • Attendance tracking
  • Results, grading and printable report cards
  • Fee records and online payment through Paystack
  • Separate portals for admins, teachers, students and parents
  • Role-based access so each person sees only what they should
  • Announcements and email notifications

How it works

Step 1

Common questions

Can the system be customised for my school?

Yes. We agree the modules your school needs first, such as attendance, results or fees, and the system is built around them.

Is student data kept private?

Access is controlled with accounts and role-based permissions, so teachers, parents and administrators only see the data they are allowed to see.

Can parents pay fees online?

Yes. Fee payments can be connected through Paystack, with records updated once a payment is confirmed.
